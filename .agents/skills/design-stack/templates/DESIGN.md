# Project Design

Version: 1
Direction: spatial
References: none

## Palette
Use a restrained neutral base with one deliberate accent. Keep foreground and
background contrast readable in both light and dark surfaces.

## Typography
Use a clear display and body hierarchy with legible sizes and line lengths.
Choose type for the project's voice and keep labels readable at small sizes.

## Layout
Give content a strong grid, generous breathing room, and clear depth cues.
Keep navigation and primary actions predictable across screen sizes.

## Motion
Use short, purposeful transitions to show spatial relationships. Respect
reduced-motion preferences and never make motion necessary to understand state.

## Accessibility
Use semantic controls, visible focus, keyboard access, and sufficient contrast.
Keep the same information and actions available without animation or pointer use.

## 3D Intent
None. A 3D pipeline runs only after an explicit project or task request.

None.
